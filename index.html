Questions, answered.

Do I need to host the bot myself?

No. Hosted plans run on Fitter infrastructure with monitoring and updates. If you'd rather self-host, the one-time licence gives you the source and a Docker setup.

Which permissions will the bot ask for?

Only what its modules need. Every bot ships with a permissions sheet so your admins can see exactly why each scope is requested.

Is the Ledger economy bot really free?

Yes — $0. You get the full Python source (discord.py 2 + SQLite) under the MIT licence. Host it, modify it, rebrand it. Want it customised or hosted for you? That's where our paid builds come in.

Why are some bots marked "In progress"?

Sentinel (moderation) and Ledger (economy) are live today. The rest are in active development — tap "Notify me" and we'll message you the day they launch.

Can you extend a store bot later?

Yes. Any store bot can be upgraded into a custom build — you only pay for the new modules.

How does payment work?

Pay instantly by UPI at checkout — scan the QR or tap to open GPay, PhonePe or Paytm, then enter your UTR number. Custom builds are billed 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. Questions? Call +91 98765 43210.

Is it compliant with Discord's rules?

Every bot follows Discord's Developer Terms and uses official APIs only. No self-bots, no token grabbing, ever.
